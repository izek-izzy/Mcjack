Sika Tanzania Construction Chemicals Limited

McJack Construction Limited is an official Distributor of Sika Tanzania Construction Chemicals Limited. Sika Tanzania, a subsidiary of Sika AG, is a global leader in construction chemicals manufacturing with operations in over 100 countries and more than 300 production facilities. The company specializes in developing systems and products for bonding, sealing, damping, reinforcing, and protecting within the construction and automotive sectors.

The product portfolio includes mining solutions, waterproofing systems, concrete admixtures, flooring systems, injection materials, roofing solutions, sealants, and other specialty construction chemicals.
