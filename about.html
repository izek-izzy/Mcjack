About McJack

Established on February 12, 2018, McJack Construction and Suppliers Limited is a 100% Zambian-owned multi-disciplinary contracting and supply company. We combine engineering precision with practical delivery to serve commercial, industrial, and community clients.

Our Identity

We prioritize integrity, regulatory compliance, and long-term client relationships. Our leadership includes experienced civil engineers and project managers who ensure quality and safety across every project.

Mission & Values

Our mission is to deliver durable infrastructure and reliable procurement solutions that support Zambia's development. We value transparency, innovation, and excellence in execution.
